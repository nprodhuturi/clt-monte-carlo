A point at or below 1 passes all three cutoffs. The smallest passing n that stays passing is the line marked n*. Click the chart to jump to that n. When the line hugs the cutoff for a long stretch, random noise (about ±0.025 in skewness) decides the last failing n, so n* can move a lot with another seed. In R, repeat with a few seeds and report the range.

Show the numbers as a table

Random numbers are generated in your browser with a fixed seed per population and n, so a picture never changes between visits. They will not match R digit for digit, but the pattern and the conclusions should. Skewness and excess kurtosis use the class formulas, and the spread check compares the simulated SD with σ/√n.
